# Project Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Team member becomes unavailable | Medium | High | High | Divide work clearly and keep documents in GitHub so the other member can continue. |
| R-02 | Assignment deadline is missed | Medium | High | High | Use the Gantt chart, milestones, and regular progress checks. |
| R-03 | Git/GitHub technical problems | Medium | Medium | Medium | Learn basic Git commands and commit/push work regularly. |
| R-04 | Requirements are unclear | Medium | High | High | Review requirements before starting later SRS work. |
| R-05 | Incorrect information is entered | Low | Medium | Low | Review documents before final submission. |
| R-06 | Files are accidentally deleted | Medium | High | High | Use Git commits and GitHub as version-controlled backup. |
| R-07 | Too much work for two members | Medium | High | High | Divide tasks using the responsibility matrix and prioritize milestones. |
| R-08 | Documents and diagrams become inconsistent | Medium | Medium | Medium | Later compare UML/UI work against the approved SRS requirements. |
