# Project Risk Register

Likelihood and impact are rated from 1 (Low) to 5 (Very High). Risk score = likelihood × impact. The score helps the team decide which risks need more attention.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation / response | Owner |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| R-01 | The team may misunderstand the library's borrowing, renewal, or reservation rules. | 3 | 4 | 12 | Write down questions early and confirm the rules with the instructor or a librarian. Mark any unconfirmed rule as an assumption. | Kahsif |
| R-02 | Assignment tasks may take longer than planned. | 3 | 4 | 12 | Break work into small tasks, check progress daily, and complete the most important items before polishing. | Ahmad |
| R-03 | One member may be unavailable near the deadline. | 2 | 4 | 8 | Share files in GitHub, keep responsibilities visible, and review progress before the last day. | Both |
| R-04 | Git push or merge problems may interrupt work. | 3 | 3 | 9 | Commit small changes often, pull before starting shared work, and avoid editing the same file at the same time. | Ahmad |
| R-05 | A required folder may not appear in GitHub because empty folders are not tracked by Git. | 3 | 2 | 6 | Add a small `.gitkeep` file inside each required empty folder. | Ahmad |
| R-06 | Private student or library information may be accidentally uploaded. | 2 | 5 | 10 | Use sample data only. Never upload passwords, private records, access tokens, or personal student information. | Both |
| R-07 | The schedule may not match the instructor's actual deadline. | 3 | 4 | 12 | Confirm the deadline and replace proposed dates in the schedule before submission. | Ahmad |
| R-08 | Project documents may contradict one another. | 3 | 3 | 9 | Review the README, charter, WBS, schedule, and risk register together before final submission. | Kahsif |

## Priority
Risks R-01, R-02, R-06, and R-07 have the highest scores in this initial assessment and should be reviewed first. Reassess scores if new information becomes available.
